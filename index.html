Challenge result

What survived—and what did not

Keeper tried the obvious conclusions first. Most were rejected. What remains is narrower and more useful.

Worth validating3 items

Change propagation

How does one important change move through affected teams, systems, methods, readiness checks and client-facing execution?

Why it matters: AGAT is already changing in several different areas.

One client across specialized businesses

How does AGAT maintain a coherent client relationship and trusted information when several specialized businesses are involved?

Why it matters: specialization is visible; harmful fragmentation is not.

Capability readiness

How does AGAT prove a new capability is ready before it is offered to clients?

Why it matters: location-specific methods and licensing are visible publicly.
Rejected by the evidence3 assumptions

“AGAT has a silo problem”

Not supported. Specialization and distributed authority may be intentional strengths.

“AGAT needs one system”

Not supported. Public evidence shows specialized tools and active integration work, not proof of harmful fragmentation.

“Keeper should replace AGAT systems”

Rejected. Any Keeper role would exist only where a validated information or coordination need is proven.

07
Evidence notes

What the public model is built from

These notes help explain the reconstruction without forcing every source detail into the main assessment.

Public company footprint
AGAT publicly describes a 41-location North American network and roughly 49,000 clients worldwide. These figures are used only as scale/context indicators.
Client-facing digital tools
Public material exposes multiple specialized client portal families, including WebEARTH, WebAIR, WebOAS, WebFLUIDS, WebMINING and WebFOOD. Their existence does not establish duplication.
Quality and accreditation
Public accreditation material indicates location/test-specific scopes. This is why capability readiness is treated as a material question rather than assumed centrally.
Digital modernization
The enhanced ECOC is a strong public example of a workflow being modernized across several functions rather than evidence that a broad transformation is needed.
Operating improvement
Recent public role announcements show active attention to workflow, responsibility clarity, client experience and consistency. These are treated as existing strengths/signals, not problems.
Current limit
Keeper has no internal AGAT data, credentials, system access or private records. Internal handoffs, decision rights and readiness mechanisms remain intentionally unresolved until validated.
